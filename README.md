This project analyzes **Titan Company Limited financial performance** over 5 years through **ratio analysis**, a fully integrated **3-statement financial model**, and a **DCF valuation** benchmarked against industry peers. The goal was to assess financial health, flag risk areas, and arrive at a valuation-backed recommendation — the kind of analysis used in **financial analysis**, audit risk assessment, and corporate strategy work.

The project showcases my skills in:

1. **Financial statement analysis** and ratio-based risk assessment
2. Building a fully linked **3-statement financial model**
3. **DCF valuation, WACC estimation, and sensitivity analysis**
4. Competitor benchmarking against **Kalyan Jewellers** for strategic insight
5. Data preparation using **Power Query** and interactive **visualization in Tableau**
